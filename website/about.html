About Truber

Who we are

Truber is a Zimbabwe-first digital freight marketplace. It connects shippers who need cargo moved with transporters who have trucks and capacity.

Mission

Truber’s mission is to connect cargo with reliable, verified transport. The goal is a clear record of every booking, from the load details to proof of delivery.

Connecting Cargo. Moving Business.

Vision

In the long term, Truber aims to be a full digital freight and logistics marketplace: find transport, compare quotes, book, arrange protected payment, monitor deliveries, manage spend and work with verified providers. Regional expansion is planned later to Zambia, Botswana, Mozambique and South Africa.

Commitment

Our commitment is to verification, honest payment labels, accurate delivery records and support for both sides of a booking. Protected payment is in testing, and cash is always labelled “Not protected”.

Technology approach

Truber is planned as a mobile-first app with a responsive website for businesses. It is being built for low bandwidth and small Android phones. The app is a preview in testing.
