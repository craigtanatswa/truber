Become a transporter

Truber is a freight marketplace for Zimbabwe. Transporters register their companies and trucks, then quote on loads that fit their routes. The public preview is in testing.

Apply to join Try the preview

Why transport with Truber

Truber gives transporters a clear place to find work, quote on terms they choose and keep a record of every trip.

  • Find loads that fit your trucks

    Browse loads by capacity, truck type, location and route, then choose which ones to quote.

  • Quote on your terms

    Send a quotation with your price and conditions for the trip.

  • Clear booking and delivery records

    Keep booking terms, trip status and proof of delivery together in one record.

  • Ratings that stay with your account

    After a completed trip, ratings will stay with your account.

Your earnings

You receive the agreed amount minus 5% commission and any processing fees. Commission applies to completed trips only.

Transporter questions

How do I get verified?

Register your company, upload your verification documents and add your trucks. Truber will review the details before an account can take bookings. Verification is planned and not live yet.

What documents do I need?

You will need company verification documents, truck registration and insurance documents, and driver identification. The app preview shows where each document is added.

How much does Truber charge?

Truber charges 5% commission on completed trips, plus any processing fees, taken from the payout.

Is cash protected?

No. Cash is recorded on the booking but is not payment-protected.

Is there automated matching?

Not yet. Automated matching is coming soon; for now, transporters choose which loads to quote.
