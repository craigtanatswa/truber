How it works

Truber is built for shippers who need trucks, and for transporters who need loads. Matching isn’t automated yet. You post or search, then compare quotes and choose.

Shipper journey

  1. 1

    Post a load

    Add the pickup, destination, goods, weight, truck type, dates and any special requirements. Add an offered rate or leave it open for quotations.

  2. 2

    Get quotations

    Transporters review the load and send their quotations.

  3. 3

    Compare quotations

    Review the price and conditions from each transporter before choosing.

  4. 4

    Choose a transporter

    Select the quotation that fits the shipment.

  5. 5

    Agree the terms

    Agree the price and how you’ll pay before the trip goes ahead. Bookings aren’t live yet.

  6. 6

    Delivery with proof of delivery

    The transporter uploads a signed delivery note, photos and recipient confirmation as proof of delivery.

  7. 7

    Rate the transporter

    After delivery is confirmed, rate the transporter so the record stays with the account.

Transporter journey

  1. 1

    Register and verify your company

    Add your company details and upload verification documents for review.

  2. 2

    Add trucks and documents

    Register each truck with its registration number, type, capacity, trailer specifications, documents, insurance, current location and assigned driver.

  3. 3

    Set availability and routes

    Show when your trucks are available and the routes you prefer to run.

  4. 4

    Find loads and send quotations

    Browse loads that fit your trucks and send a quotation for the ones you want.

  5. 5

    Agree the trip

    Agree the terms with the shipper. Once bookings are live, a trip will go ahead only with an agreed payment arrangement.

  6. 6

    Update status

    Keep the trip status current as it moves from agreed to delivered.

    Trip status

    • 1Terms agreed
    • 2Truck dispatched
    • 3Goods collected
    • 4In transit
    • 5Delivered
  7. 7

    Upload proof of delivery

    Upload the signed delivery note, photos and recipient confirmation after drop-off.

  8. 8

    Payouts Planned

    Once payouts are live, you’ll receive the agreed price minus 5% commission on completed trips and any payment fees.

Matching is not automated yet. GPS tracking is coming soon.
