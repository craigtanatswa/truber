How it works

Truber is built for shippers who need trucks, and for transporters who need loads. Matching is not automated yet — you post or search, then quote and book.

For shippers

  1. 1

    Post a load

    Tell us the pickup, destination, goods, weight, truck type, dates and any special requirements. Add an offered rate, or leave it open for quotations.

  2. 2

    Compare quotations

    Transporters review your load and send quotes. You compare prices and conditions, then choose the one that fits.

  3. 3

    Confirm the booking

    Agree the price and how you’ll pay before the trip is booked. A booking is only confirmed once both sides have agreed the terms.

  4. 4

    Confirm delivery

    The transporter uploads proof of delivery. You check it, confirm, and rate the trip.

For transporters

  1. 1

    Register and verify

    Add your company and trucks, then upload your documents for review. Verified accounts will be able to take bookings.

  2. 2

    Find loads and quote

    Browse loads that fit your trucks and send a quotation. There is no automated matching yet — you choose which loads to quote.

  3. 3

    Run the trip

    Once booked, collect the goods and update the status as the trip moves: dispatched, collected, in transit, delivered. Live GPS tracking is coming soon.

  4. 4

    Upload proof and get paid

    Upload proof of delivery after the drop-off. On completed trips, Truber’s commission is 5%. You receive the agreed amount minus that commission and any payment charges.

Checked, not just claimed

Trust on Truber comes from documents, ratings and a clear record of the booking — not from slogans.

  • Verified companies, trucks and documents

    Companies, trucks and supporting documents will be reviewed before an account can post loads or take bookings.

    Planned

  • Ratings

    After a completed trip, shippers and transporters will be able to rate each other. Ratings will stay with the account.

    Planned

  • Dispute handling

    If something goes wrong, either side will be able to raise a dispute for Truber to review.

    Planned

  • Deals outside Truber can lead to a ban

    Arranging a deal off the platform can lead to a ban. Keep bookings on Truber.

  • Cash is not protected

    Cash agreed on a Truber booking is not protected. Always treat cash as “Not protected”.

    Not protected

  • Protected payment is in testing

    Protected payment is in testing and is not live. Truber does not hold funds. The plan is for an authorised independent provider to hold payment until delivery is confirmed and Truber approves the payout.

Pricing

5% on completed trips only

There is no fee to sign up, and no fee to list a load or a truck. Truber charges a 5% commission only when a trip is completed.

Example

On a completed trip priced at US$1,000.00, Truber’s commission is US$50.00. The transporter’s amount before payment processing charges is US$950.00. Payment processing charges, if any, are shown separately and are not included in that 5%.

Important: Cash bookings are recorded on Truber but are not payment-protected.

Frequently asked questions

Is Truber live?

No. Truber is a preview in testing. You can explore the planned flow in the app preview. Verification, live payments and Protected payment are not live yet.

Who is Truber for?

Truber is for shippers and transporters, starting in Zimbabwe.

What does it cost?

Truber charges 5% on completed trips only. There is no fee to sign up or to list a load or truck.

Is my payment protected?

Protected payment is in testing and is not live. Cash is not protected.

Do you offer GPS tracking?

GPS tracking is coming soon.

When is a booking confirmed?

A booking is confirmed only once a payment arrangement is confirmed.

Where do you operate?

The planned pilot is Harare to Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru and Masvingo.
