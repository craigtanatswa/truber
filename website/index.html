How it works

Truber is built for shippers who need trucks, and for transporters who need loads. Matching isn’t automated yet. You post or search, then compare quotes and choose.

  1. 01

    Post a load

    Tell us the pickup, destination, goods, weight, truck type, dates and any special requirements. Add an offered rate, or leave it open for quotations.

  2. 02

    Compare quotations

    Transporters review your load and send quotes. You compare prices and conditions, then choose the one that fits.

  3. 03

    Agree the terms

    Agree the price and how you’ll pay before the trip goes ahead.

  4. 04

    Confirm delivery

    The transporter uploads proof of delivery. You check it and confirm delivery.

Pricing

5% on completed trips only

There is no fee to sign up, and no fee to list a load or a truck. Truber will charge a 5% commission only when a trip is completed.

Example

On a completed trip priced at US$1,000.00, Truber’s commission is US$50.00. The transporter’s amount before payment fees is US$950.00. Payment fees, if any, are shown separately and are not included in that 5%.

Important: Cash trips will be recorded on Truber but are Not protected. Protected payment is coming soon, and Truber doesn’t hold any funds today. When bookings open, no booking is confirmed without a confirmed payment arrangement.

Frequently asked questions

Is Truber live?

No. The web app is a test preview. Bookings and payments are not live. Verification is planned and in testing. Protected payment is coming soon, and Truber doesn’t hold any funds today. GPS tracking is coming soon.

What does it cost?

Truber will charge 5% on completed trips only. There is no fee to sign up or to list a load or truck. Payment fees are separate.

Is my payment protected?

Protected payment is coming soon, and Truber doesn’t hold any funds today. Cash is Not protected.

Do you offer GPS tracking?

GPS tracking is coming soon.

When will a trip be confirmed?

Bookings aren’t live yet. When they are, a trip will be confirmed only once a payment arrangement is agreed.

Where do you operate?

The planned pilot is Harare to Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru and Masvingo.
