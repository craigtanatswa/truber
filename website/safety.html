Safety

Safety and trust

Truber is being built around verification, clear delivery records and honest payment labels.

Checked before a trip

Verification is planned for the companies, drivers and trucks on a trip.

  • Verified companies

    Company details and verification documents will be reviewed before an account can post loads or take bookings.

    Planned

  • Verified drivers

    Driver identification is planned to be recorded and checked as part of the trip record.

    Planned

  • Verified trucks

    Registration, documents and insurance checks are planned as part of truck verification.

    Planned

Proof of delivery

The transporter uploads a signed delivery note, photos and recipient confirmation. The shipper checks the record and confirms delivery.

Ratings

After a completed trip, shippers and transporters will be able to rate each other. Ratings will stay with the account.

Planned

Disputes

If something goes wrong, either side will be able to raise a dispute for Truber to review.
