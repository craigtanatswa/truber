Safety and trust

Truber is built around verification, clear delivery records and honest payment labels.

Checked before a booking

Verification is part of the Truber model for the companies, drivers and trucks on a trip.

  • Verified companies

    Company details and verification documents are reviewed before an account can post loads or take bookings.

  • Verified drivers

    Driver identification is recorded and checked as part of the trip record.

  • Verified trucks

    Registration, documents and insurance checks are part of truck verification.

Proof of delivery

The transporter uploads a signed delivery note, photos and recipient confirmation. The shipper checks the record and confirms delivery.

Ratings

After a completed trip, shippers and transporters can rate each other. Ratings stay with the account.

Disputes

If something goes wrong, either side can raise a dispute for Truber to review.
