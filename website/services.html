Services

Services

Post loads, quote, agree terms and keep a record of every trip in the Truber web app.

  • Load posting and quotations

    Shippers post loads with pickup, destination, goods type, weight, truck type and dates, then compare the quotations they receive.

  • Trip management

    Confirm the booking once a payment arrangement is agreed, then follow the trip status from truck dispatched to delivered.

  • Proof of delivery records

    Keep signed delivery notes, photos and recipient confirmation with the trip.

  • Ratings and reviews

    Rate a transporter or shipper after a completed trip. Ratings stay with the account.

  • Disputes

    Only the shipper can open a dispute, within 24 hours of confirming delivery. Truber reviews it if you can’t settle it between you.
